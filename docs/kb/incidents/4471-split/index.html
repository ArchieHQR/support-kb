How to tell them apart

  • parser: the tenant writes the day first, and the date came in by app, email or the booking-v2 form. Both numbers are 12 or lower, and day and month swap: 7 April arrives as 4 July.
  • hint: the same swap, but only through the booking-v3 form. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field and then reads the date day first. People type what the hint tells them. Fixing the parser leaves this as it is: the form has to print the order it reads.
  • shift: no swap. The date moves back one day (Tuesday becomes Monday), or recipients in two time zones see two different days. The desk already noted these as one-day moves. They are a time zone bug, not a date-order bug.

Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser. The tag is a guess. The tenant’s locale and booking form decide the cause, so triage by the tenant.
